## Assessment contract, confidence and coverage

The additive `assessment.risk_indicators` states are
`NO_SIGNIFICANT_CONCERNS_OBSERVED`, `SOME_CONCERNS_OBSERVED`, `ELEVATED_CONCERN`,
and `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE_TO_ASSESS`. A reassuring observed-content label requires
informative readable opportunity content and recorded content-check execution.
A URL string alone, empty/sparse input or missing historical execution metadata cannot
qualify merely because no scanner finding exists.

`assessment.authenticity` distinguishes `UNVERIFIED`, `VERIFICATION_UNAVAILABLE`,
`SCOPED_CORROBORATION`, and `INDEPENDENT_CONTRADICTION`. Positive/negative independent
support is limited to cited claims. `offer_status` remains UNVERIFIED; company existence,
search snippets, matching notice fields, submitted duplicates and completed content
rules cannot authenticate a specific offer, recruiter or payment destination. Legacy
`state`, `risk_score`, `offer_authenticity` and fusion contracts remain available.

The existing fusion score formula, prior, severity ratios, thresholds and correlation
rules were not changed. Under the default heuristic mode the index is decision support,
not a calibrated probability. The existing confidence calculation uses distinct eligible
correlation groups: `round(min(95, groups*18) * selected_rule_reliability * conflict_factor)`;
the conflict factor is 0.7 when applicable. It measures neither parsing success nor
percentage of genuine offers. UI wording now says **Evidence reliability (heuristic)**.
Zero can mean there is no contributing risk/reference evidence, while parsing succeeds.

Coverage is the rounded fraction of recorded completed check slots over recorded
applicable slots, with actual criteria and unavailable reasons. Content checks contribute
to that denominator, so coverage is not independent-authentication coverage. Input types
are optional and are not a required-input checklist. Historical absent counters remain
unknown rather than fabricated. Processing counts, extracted claims, coverage,
corroboration and observed risk are shown separately.

## Five judge questions and accurate answers

1. **How can low concern still be unverified?** Risk indicators describe patterns in
   available material. Authenticity requires independent support for a precisely
   scoped claim. Missing warnings cannot establish sender/offer authorization.
2. **Can an LLM decide the score or invent a source?** No. Core scans, fusion, graph,
   recommendations and fallback work without one. The optional provider contract
   permits bounded understanding/explanation; validated citations do not grant scoring
   or evidence authority.
3. **What stops repeated posters increasing certainty?** Source/claim/evidence
   provenance and correlation groups, duplicate tracking and strongest-per-group
   fusion. The regression suite compares duplicates with a single source.
4. **Does matching an official advertisement prove the recruiter?** No. Only cited
   comparable fields can gain scoped corroboration. Recruiter authorization, file
   authenticity and the payment destination remain separate unresolved questions.
5. **Is the 100% benchmark score your fraud accuracy?** No. It is a synthetic
   development regression result on 45 examples. It has no held-out real-world validation
   or probability calibration; 29 examples explicitly abstain.
